## A — Art terrace (recommended)

Visual thesis: finish the product story with a generous illustration that has its own breathing room; then offer one decisive next step and a quiet, orderly service rail. Art and utilities have distinct areas, so neither becomes a crowded banner.

Layout and spacing: a full-width illustration terrace within the product's page-width frame, generous inset and deliberate clear edges; roughly21:9 desktop framing, an art-directed taller crop on390px. Under it, an editorial CTA row: headline and short evidence-aware copy left, one obvious action right. A ruled utility rail follows with native disclosures for AI, Updates and Feedback. Curated studio links form a restrained separate row; native legal/navigation closes the frame. At768px the CTA wraps naturally. At390px art remains substantial, headline/body/action stack, utility labels stay readable and disclosures open below their own labels rather than covering content. Do not replace the illustration with a tiny thumbnail to fit the rail.

Typography: product display type for the closing headline—Atlas Georgia with selective italic emphasis, CodeVetter's incumbent sans. Comfortable two-line display scale; readable sans body; small mono evidence/provenance label. Utility text stays normal readable size, without rows of miniature all-caps labels.

Semantic color: large paper/night or true-black art stage uses the product's established tokens. CTA action uses existing product action color; utility surfaces are quiet relative to the CTA. Explicit focus/status/error tokens adapt to actual light/dark context. No bright attention color on every utility link.

Interaction: one CTA remains visible. AI/Updates/Feedback are ordinary page-flow disclosure rows; only the chosen service opens, while forms retain values and explicit consent. Feedback opens the maintained dialog from its page-flow support trigger. Basic native disclosure behavior or a proven existing primitive; no new menu/dialog runtime. Keyboard opening, focus return and network retry states remain clear. Discovery can use short curated ordinary links rather than requiring a moving marquee to discover products; retain catalog/referral behavior.

Product-native signature: a broad labelled illustration with one small provenance/intent caption at its edge—Atlas evidence cabinet, CodeVetter execution bench—followed by a disciplined terrace-to-rail transition.

Landing/app continuity: marketing gives art and CTA full prominence. Atlas app footer keeps the same frame/art vocabulary and service hierarchy, but shortens vertical crop and reduces display type; place it after task content, never between records and inspector. On narrow app screens it remains reachable in document flow without covering the return control.

Deliberate risk: one more disclosure step for optional services. Justified because the primary path is faster to scan and art stays generous. Mitigate with literal labels, a visible support affordance, adequate targets and a clear expanded state.

## B — Studio workbench

Visual thesis: a working editorial station where product art anchors one side and an organized service index gives the closing area a useful, browsable rhythm. This is an asymmetric reading layout, not the terrace's horizontal layers.

Layout and spacing: desktop left side is a tall, substantial art panel with a concise product caption; right side starts with a shorter editorial headline/copy and one CTA, then a vertical numbered service index (Ask AI, Updates, Feedback). Below or alongside the index, studio projects are readable ruled rows with product name and purpose, not a generic grid of cards. Native links/legal occupy the workbench's bottom rule. Around768px keep the asymmetry only while both text and art remain comfortable; otherwise move art above the editorial column. At390px one generous art panel leads, followed by CTA, vertical utilities and studio rows. It must not turn into three columns of cramped tiny text.

Typography: editorial mixed-scale rhythm: PH serif headline and compact mono source caption; CodeVetter native sans headline and mono execution caption. Project names have a stronger weight than their descriptions. Service index is readable body size, with numbers used for orientation rather than suggesting a required workflow.

Semantic color: illustration panel and content desk use two established product surfaces; the separating rule provides grouping. A selected utility uses a restrained action/focus accent. Studio descriptions use a readable muted token, not low-contrast grey. Light/dark role changes keep the panel separation intentional rather than simply invert colors.

Interaction: services form a vertical index with native disclosure expansion directly below the selected label. A desktop selection may expand within the service column without moving art; mobile stays in normal flow. Use the same maintained AI links, consented form and feedback dialog as A. Project rows are ordinary links, usable without hover or animation. Preserve input content when a different row is inspected; avoid tab semantics unless actual existing accessible tab behavior is intentionally reused.

Product-native signature: art resembles a larger specimen/workbench plate with a tiny coordinate/source caption, paired with the visible evidence-oriented index. CodeVetter's illustrated bench and actual workflow copy make this specific; PH's cabinet/source-slip art and local-workflow disclosure provide a distinct interpretation.

Landing/app continuity: retain the same asymmetric frame and ruled index in landing and Atlas app; app reduces display scale and chooses a shorter panel crop, while keeping record navigation clearly separate from footer utilities. CodeVetter native counterpart remains unchanged.

Deliberate risk: the index and project rows may feel denser and more operational than a landing's final persuasive moment. Justified for users who value orderly exploration. Mitigate with generous art, visible single CTA, enough vertical spacing and only a few curated projects; do not surface the entire portfolio at once.

## C — Closing scene

Visual thesis: a product-specific illustrated final scene with clear title/action boundaries, followed by a compact utility bar and a typographic studio index. The art is the event; tools stay in a separate quiet afterword.

Layout and spacing: a short title/copy/CTA header above a full-bleed art stage; art occupies its own wide closing scene with no utility cards sitting over it. A controlled edge caption marks the illustration as conceptual when applicable. Beneath the stage, a compact horizontal disclosure bar for AI, Updates and Feedback spans the frame; expanded service content appears below the bar in document flow. Studio projects are a text index, grouped by concise purpose rather than a card wall, with native links/legal at the bottom. At390px retain an art-directed scene crop with the product-native subject visible; header and lower controls wrap into clear rows. No overlay text on a busy illustration and no horizontal control scrolling required to find Feedback.

Typography: confident larger closing headline with sparse support copy; PH retains Atlas serif, CodeVetter retains its sans. Small mono labels are limited to the scene caption and index markers. Studio names form a typographic list with generous line-height. The headline may be larger than A, but never compress utility labels to compensate.

Semantic color: the scene may occupy the product's deeper night/black surface; header and lower bar retain explicitly contrasting product surfaces. Action/focus tokens remain readable. Art's decorative highlight colors never become the only indication of a control's state. Light and dark variants change stage/surface relationships, not the artwork's purpose.

Interaction: the header owns the single visible next-step CTA; compact lower service disclosures reveal the same maintained providers/form/support interactions. Scene itself is static and non-interactive, with no parallax, canvas, video or pointer effect required. Keep text/index fully useful if the asset fails. Prefer reduced-motion-safe content and ordinary semantic links over effect-driven discovery.

Product-native signature: an original wide research/verification scene becomes the last memorable product impression—Atlas evidence drawers/source trails, CodeVetter bounded execution/evidence routing. The clear before/after art boundaries distinguish this from a generic background behind a call-to-action.

Landing/app continuity: landing uses the full scene. Atlas app uses a shallower panoramic version of the same composition after its work area; title becomes a calm task-support statement, while utilities remain equally available. Footer must not become taller than the preceding app's useful content on390px. Native CodeVetter remains its existing platform UI.

Deliberate risk: the dramatic scene can consume vertical space and make utilities appear distant, especially in app mode. Justified if the owner prioritizes a memorable finish. Mitigate with a controlled app crop, explicit readable utility bar, asset dimensions reserved before loading, and no cinematic motion dependency.
