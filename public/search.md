# Advanced anime search — Anime List

Canonical: https://anime.significanthobbies.com/search

Pick a genre and season, then open More filters to set a minimum score or choose a format. Lower Popularity to include lesser-known titles. Search without an account; share your filters by copying the page URL.

For example, choose Action and add a score filter greater than or equal to 7 to narrow the catalog. The default popularity threshold is 100,000 MyAnimeList members; member counts measure catalog popularity, not whether a title fits your taste. Try a lower threshold when you want less widely watched anime.
