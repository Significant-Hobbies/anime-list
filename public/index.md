## What you get

### Filter across every dimension

Score, type, genre, theme, year, airing status, member count — stack operators (includes all/any, excludes, numeric comparisons) until the results match what you want. Filter state lives in the URL, so any query is shareable.

### A watchlist that's yours

Track titles as Watching, Completed, Deferred, Avoiding, or BRR. Private to your Google account, synced across sessions, and never sold.

### Ranking that surfaces hidden gems

A log-scale popularity formula keeps mega-popular titles from burying everything else, so quality scores from smaller fandoms still get a fair shot.

## Frequently asked

Where does the data come from?

Catalog data comes from MyAnimeList through the public Tenrai API. We are not affiliated with MyAnimeList.net or Tenrai.

How often is it updated?

A daily sync at midnight UTC refreshes the current and previous anime seasons plus the top manga pages. A quarterly job re-scores the full catalog.

Do I need an account?

No — search, stats, and the schedule work fully signed-out. Sign in with Google only if you want a personal watchlist.

How many titles does Anime List cover?

The unfiltered anime and manga totals below come from our retained catalog, rather than the full MyAnimeList catalog. Search defaults to anime with at least 100,000 members; remove that filter to include lesser-known titles.

Is it free?

Yes. No paid tier, no ads. The point is to explore the catalog without anything in the way.
